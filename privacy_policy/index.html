Akoli Speech

PRIVACY POLICY

A personalised speech recognition application for individuals with non-standard speech

Provided by University College London — Global Disability Innovation Hub (GDI Hub)

Effective Date: 15 August 2026 | Version: 1.1


Version 1.1 - 8 September 2026 - Added Singapore to supported regions.

1. Introduction

University College London's Global Disability Innovation Hub ("UCL GDI Hub", "we", "us", or "our") is the controller responsible for your personal data when you use the Akoli Speech application ("the App" or "Akoli Speech"). We are a research centre based at University College London, Gower Street, London WC1E 6BT, United Kingdom.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect when you use the App, how and why we use it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and what rights you have over it. It should be read alongside our Terms of Service, which govern your use of the App more generally. Terms used in this Policy have the same meaning as in the Terms of Service unless stated otherwise.

Akoli Speech is an extension from the Project Euphonia open-source toolkit (https://github.com/google/project-euphonia-app), made available under the Apache 2.0 licence. This Policy covers GDI Hub's processing of your personal data through the compiled App as we distribute it; it does not cover any separate application that a third party builds using the underlying open-source code.

By downloading, installing, or using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with how we process your personal data, you must not use the App.

2. Who This Policy Applies To

This Policy applies to individuals who download, install, or use the App ("Users" or "you"). The app is currently available to people residing in Ghana, India, Uganda, Kenya, Rwanda, Colombia, Brazil, the United States of America, Canada, the United Kingdom, Singapore, and countries within the European Economic Area (EEA) and wider Europe (the "Supported Territories").

3. Personal Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

Category What it includes Source
Speech + Transcript Data Audio recordings of your voice and the associated text transcriptions, collected as you record phrases to build and use your remote Personalised Speech Model. Provided directly by you through the App
Account and profile information Basic identifiers needed to set up and secure your account, such as email address. Provided directly by you
Usage data Information about how you interact with the App's Record, Train, and Use modes, App version, crash logs, and diagnostic data. Generated automatically by the App
Device information Device type, operating system, and language settings, used to ensure the App functions correctly on your device. Generated automatically by the App
Correspondence Records of communications between you and GDI Hub, for example when you contact us with a support request or to exercise a data right. Provided directly by you

4. How We Use Your Personal Data

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • To create, train, and improve your Personalised Speech Model from your Speech Data.
  • To provide the App's core features: create a dataset that can be used to train your personalized speech model (Record), train your personalized speech model (Train), and transcribe your speech (Use).
  • To operate, maintain, and secure the App and its underlying infrastructure, including diagnosing technical issues.
  • To communicate with you, including responding to support requests and data rights requests, and notifying you of material changes to our Terms or this Policy.
  • To understand, in aggregate and wherever possible using de-identified or anonymised data, how the App is used, so that we can improve its accuracy and accessibility for the wider community of individuals with non-standard speech.
  • To comply with our legal obligations and to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

We do not use your Speech Data or other personal data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal data.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

Where the UK GDPR, EU GDPR, or an equivalent data protection law applies to our processing of your personal data, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract — to provide you with the App and its features under our Terms of Service.
  • Legitimate interests — for limited purposes such as maintaining the security of the App and understanding aggregate, de-identified usage patterns to improve the service, where these interests are not overridden by your rights.

For Users in Ghana, India, Kenya, Uganda, Rwanda, Colombia, the United States, and other Supported Territories, we process your personal data in accordance with the equivalent lawful bases available under the applicable local data protection law.

6. Consent

By registering and signing in to the app, you consent to our terms of services, and privacy policy. If at some point in time you decide to withdraw your consent from the app, please do contact us using the details in Section 14. If you withdraw consent, we will stop processing your Speech Data going forward and will delete it in accordance with your request, but you will no longer be able to use the App, since your Personalised Speech Model cannot function without it.

7. How We Store and Protect Your Data

Your Speech Data and other personal data are stored in cloud-based infrastructure (Google Firebase Storage or an equivalent service in Europe for GDPR) controlled by GDI Hub or its designated technical partners. Data is encrypted both in transit and at rest.

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures designed to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction, and we review these measures periodically as the App and its infrastructure evolve. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Access to the user's Speech Data and Personalised Speech Model within GDI Hub is limited to personnel and technical partners who need it to operate, maintain, or improve the App upon explicit permissions of the user.

8. International Data Transfers

Because the App is available across the Supported Territories and our cloud infrastructure may process data outside your country of residence, your personal data may be transferred to, stored, and processed in countries other than the one in which you live, including the United Kingdom and other countries where our infrastructure or technical partners operate.

Where we transfer personal data out of the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area, we do so on the basis of an adequacy decision, or by using appropriate safeguards recognised under the UK GDPR or EU GDPR.

9. Who We Share Your Data With

We do not sell your personal data. We share personal data only in the following circumstances:

  • Technical service providers — cloud storage and infrastructure providers (such as Firebase or equivalent providers) who process data on our behalf, under contractual obligations to protect it and use it only as we instruct.
  • Legal and safety purposes — where required to comply with a legal obligation, court order, or lawful request from a public authority, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of GDI Hub, our users, or others.

We do not share your identifiable Speech Data with third parties for their own independent marketing or commercial purposes.

10. Data Retention

We retain your Speech Data and Personalised Speech Model for as long as your account remains active and you continue to use the App.

If you request deletion of your data, we will delete your Speech Data and Personalised Speech Model within 30 days of receiving a valid request, except to the extent we are required to retain certain information by law or to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims, in which case we retain only what is necessary and for no longer than required.

Usage and diagnostic data that does not identify you may be retained for longer periods in aggregated or de-identified form to help us understand and improve the App.

11. Your Rights Over Your Data

You retain ownership of your Speech Data at all times. You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Request deletion of your Speech Data, Personalised Speech Model, and other personal data.
  • Object to, or request that we restrict, certain processing of your personal data.
  • Request a copy of your personal data in a portable format, where technically feasible.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 14. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

12. Children

The App is intended for use by individuals who are at least 18 years of age, or the age of majority in their country of residence, in line with our Terms of Service. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in law, best practice, or the functionality of the App. Where we make material changes, we will notify you through the App or by other reasonable means before the changes take effect. The "Effective Date" at the top of this Policy indicates when it was last revised. Your continued use of the App after a change takes effect constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.
