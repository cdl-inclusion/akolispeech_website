2. Definitions

“Non-standard speech” refers to speech patterns that may be difficult for others or standard speech recognition technology to understand. This may result from conditions such as cerebral palsy, motor neurone disease (ALS/MND), stroke, Down syndrome, Parkinson’s disease, traumatic brain injury, or other neurological, developmental, or acquired conditions affecting speech production.

“Personalised Speech Model” means the customised automatic speech recognition (ASR) model created from your recorded speech data, trained to recognise your individual speech patterns.

“Speech Data” means the audio recordings and associated text transcriptions you provide through the App for the purpose of creating your Personalised Speech Model.

“User” or “you” means any individual who downloads, installs, or uses the App.

“Supported Territories” means the countries and regions where the App is currently available, including Ghana, India, Uganda, Kenya, Rwanda, Colombia, the United States of America, Canada, the United Kingdom, Brazil, and countries within the European Economic Area (EEA) and wider Europe.

4. Description of the Service

Akoli Speech is a mobile application that uses machine learning to create a personalised speech recognition model tailored to your unique voice and speech patterns. The App provides the following core features:

  • Listen (Transcribe): Transcribes your speech into on-screen text, serving as live captioning for your voice.
  • Repeat (Restate): Restates what you have said using a clear, synthesised voice to support face-to-face communication.
  • Type (Voice Typing): Enables you to use your personalised speech model as a keyboard input in other applications, allowing you to compose messages, documents, and other text using your voice.

4.1 Multi-language Support
