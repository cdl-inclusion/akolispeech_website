Akoli Speech Data Removal Options

Users can request full data and account deletion. To request data and account deletion, please send an email support@akolispeech.com from the email address associated with your Akoli Speech account. Additionally provide your Akoli Speech user id, visible on the profile page. Please note that once account and data deletion has been requested, it is irreversible. Users can also delete individual utterances (one by one) directly from the app.
